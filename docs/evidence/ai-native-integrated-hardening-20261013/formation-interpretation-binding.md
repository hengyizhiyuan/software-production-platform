# Formation: owner-local interpretation and role binding

2026-10-13. This implements the approved Integrated Hardening Phase 2 sequence
inside WORK_FULFILLMENT_PROJECTION. It does not introduce another Owner, IR,
coordinator, formal Wire version, authority or budget.

## Observed common boundary

Development inventories (not Holdout) repeatedly reached complete Provider
responses, but the complete route graph mixed source omissions, conflicting or
duplicate contributions, proof-role errors and incomplete scope operands.
Local edits execute, and the format/source repair mechanisms are useful, but
have not established semantic convergence. No evidence shows the final
Validator wrongly accepting or rejecting the examined outputs.

The previous temporary representation still asked a single response to interpret
all sources, decide components, select consumers and bind proof operands. Renaming
fields did not separate those responsibilities. This is the current engineering
boundary; another alias, error label or unchanged model rerun would not resolve it.

## Minimal implementation within the existing Owner

1. The first Formation candidate may contain `components`: exact source references,
   optional exact component quotes, inspectable understanding claims, alternatives
   and unknowns. The complete immutable inventory and cross-source context remain
   available. There is no evidence, permission or accepted meaning in these claims.
2. Owner observes identity, raw claim fingerprint and quote geometry separately.
   Safe malformed claim fields and unknown references remain explicit UNKNOWN;
   they are not coerced or expanded. Unexpected authority fields, unsafe data,
   duplicate JSON keys or an unbound representation do not gain recovery rights.
3. The original one feedback boundary binds original response bytes, request and
   response receipts, Attempt and inventory. The remaining Formation candidate
   supplies the first complete role/proof plan, independently reconsidering claims
   against the authoritative original sources. Owner provides current header and
   unique geometry, not semantic choices or supporting evidence.
4. Every original formal coverage, source/Fact type, scope, permission, proof,
   consumer and independent semantic Review predicate runs unchanged. A complete
   lawful method plan still does not prove the artifact is implemented or give
   Guardian/Human authority. Missing methods remain UNRESOLVED and stop truthfully.

Temporary negotiation: `owner-local-interpretation-role-construction-v4`.
Formal `fulfillment-compact-v1`, accepted Fact and Binding contracts are unchanged.
Legitimate existing full Wire and semantic route representations remain consumable;
this is not an exclusive external shape. Older persisted requests retain their
original negotiation/defaults and terminal stops.

The original limits remain 2 Formation candidates, 1 feedback, at most 1 independent
Review per valid candidate and 4 logical calls. This internal path spends its two
Formation opportunities on understanding and complete role binding; it does not
reset those limits or add a retry after a failed second plan. Review only runs when
the final complete plan is deterministically eligible.

## Qualification and truth boundary

Controlled tests cover small/medium/complex inventories, multiple components and
alternatives, invalid or unobserved understanding claims, explicit UNKNOWN,
source/path/proof errors, missing contributions, duplicate routes, fabricated
Fact dependencies and parent drift. PostgreSQL checkpoints cover negotiated
v1/v2/v3/v4, replay and observation of the second response before a process stop;
restart calls only the remaining independent Review.

The initial three failed assertions read a nonexistent Binding field in the test;
the failure receipt is retained, and the assertion now reads the actual formation
receipt. Runtime was not relaxed. Controlled success does not qualify a real
Provider or close C3. Exact source/image and non-Holdout live results are recorded
separately after freeze. Holdout has not been read or used for implementation.

## Remaining boundary

The module has not proved reliable production convergence under the fixed Provider
profile. Actual semantic construction and Review, then normal user G0 and independent
qualification, are still required. Trace product convergence is separately real
qualified; it cannot substitute for this P0 boundary or C3 Closure.
