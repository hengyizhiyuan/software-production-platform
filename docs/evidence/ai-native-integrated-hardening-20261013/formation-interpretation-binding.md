inside WORK_FULFILLMENT_PROJECTION. It does not introduce another Owner, IR,

## Observed common boundary

Development inventories (not Holdout) repeatedly reached complete Provider
responses, but the complete route graph mixed source omissions, conflicting or
duplicate contributions, proof-role errors and incomplete scope operands.
Local edits execute, and the format/source repair mechanisms are useful, but
have not established semantic convergence. No evidence shows the final
Validator wrongly accepting or rejecting the examined outputs.

The previous temporary representation still asked a single response to interpret
all sources, decide components, select consumers and bind proof operands. Renaming
fields did not separate those responsibilities. This is the current engineering
boundary; another alias, error label or unchanged model rerun would not resolve it.
