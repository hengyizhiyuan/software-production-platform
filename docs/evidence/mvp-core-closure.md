## Closed Core Boundary

The closed Core includes:

- Motive intake and long-lived Work Admission separated from Production
  Admission;
- reconstructable Steering Plan, revisions, Steps, decisions, governed
  semantic `DESIGN`/`REFINE`, `PRODUCE`, `VERIFY_ACCEPT`, and `COMPLETE`;
- real schema-constrained semantic Provider execution over persisted context;
- PLAN-1B `ONE_PWU_FIT`, SPG admission, isolated Executor Attempt, bounded
  self-refine, independent Completion and Verification, and Candidate sealing;
- exact Human Candidate Authorization, Repository Integration, Runtime Commit,
  Trusted Baseline advancement, and active-Runtime convergence;
- truthful Human Attention, STOP/BLOCKED, completed Work, and trusted-result
  projections.

[Dogfood #9](dogfood/long-lived-motive-dogfood-9-production-loop-closure.md)
proves the fresh machine-side complete production loop.
[Dogfood #10](dogfood/long-lived-motive-dogfood-10-human-acceptance.md) proves
the Human-operated loop, exact Trusted Baseline activation, and bounded Human
functional acceptance. Historical Dogfood failures remain unchanged and retain
their original evidence value.

## Explicit Non-blocking Deferrals

Core closure does not claim completion of:

- `SEMANTIC_EXECUTION_OBSERVABILITY_GAP` — **CONFIRMED / PARTIALLY MITIGATED /
  NON-BLOCKING**; post-completion execution-history visibility is post-Core
  Product Experience work;
- `VERIFICATION_RUNTIME_COVERAGE_GAP` — **OPEN / NON-BLOCKING / DEFERRED**;
- Multi-PWU production / PLAN-1A — **DEFERRED OUTSIDE CURRENT CORE**;
- duration-aware PWU sizing and capacity-aware decomposition — **DEFERRED**;
- durable execution supervision, heartbeat, and stall recovery —
  **DEFERRED_BY_MVP**;
- complete R4 maintenance-lineage recovery and R4-D execution —
  **DEFERRED_BY_MVP**, with the historical Recovery Barrier preserved;
- further UI/Product Experience — **POST-CORE**;
- Linux deployment and promotion — **POST-LOCAL-MVP**;
- ECF, full Guardian, Interpretation Externalization, Executor fleet, HA, and
  multi-user governance — future or separately admitted capabilities.

The next architecture topic may calibrate duration-aware PWU sizing and
capacity-aware decomposition, but this closure neither designs nor authorizes
that work.
