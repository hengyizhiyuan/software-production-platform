# Course-table Domain Grounding and the Semantic Truth Boundary

Date: 2026-09-20

Status: **PRESERVED FUTURE WIC INPUT / NON-BLOCKING**

## Case

Human Dogfood used a primary-school course-table page and the expression `8×5`.
Engineering Semantic Truth correctly demonstrated the production-critical
capabilities needed by Watt: a reversible interpretation of eight course
periods across five weekdays, exact Human-source provenance, and later explicit
supersession when the Human changed the meaning to eight columns and five rows.
Production and Verification consumed the admitted fact identities rather than
independently reinterpreting the original phrase.

The produced page could still differ from the Human's expected course-table
visual convention. Conventional orientation, header placement, time-axis
presentation, familiar school timetable structure, and other domain-shaped
choices require software-domain grounding and product judgment beyond retaining
the cardinalities themselves.

## Architecture lesson

This mismatch is not evidence that Engineering Semantic Truth failed. Semantic
Truth answers what Watt currently knows or believes, with authority,
epistemic status, provenance, and supersession. It does not guarantee that an
Executor or design policy knows every domain convention implied by those facts.

The case is therefore future input to WIC Software Production SOP × LLM and
Software Domain Grounding. A future design should determine how Watt obtains,
challenges, and verifies domain conventions without turning the fact ledger into
a universal ontology or adding a course-table-specific parser.
