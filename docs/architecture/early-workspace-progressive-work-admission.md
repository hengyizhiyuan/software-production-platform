# Early Workspace and Progressive Work Admission — Human Review V1

Status: implemented candidate for Human review; acceptance and closure pending.

This slice extends [Work Interaction & Closed-loop Refinement](work-interaction-closed-loop-refinement.md) and preserves [Repository Asset and Managed Execution Workspace](repository-asset-and-managed-execution-workspace.md). Motive remains the Human's intended outcome; Work remains its governed representation. Steering owns WHAT NEXT and the Executor owns HOW.

## Early Workspace

A meaningful BUILD/ACTION_REQUEST/MODIFY/DEPLOY interpretation opens the existing four-surface Workspace from the current Interaction assessment. A direct informational question does not. This is a **prospective projection**, not a persisted Work, new lifecycle state, PWU, plan, production authorization, or fabricated engineering source. Reality states that no source is bound; Agenda offers a high-level orientation rather than an invented governed plan; Production is IDLE; Actions presents only currently available Human choices. Conversation and refinement remain available in the same Workspace. Admission changes its basis from candidate Interaction to governed Work without changing the four-surface mental model.

## Next-step sufficiency

`READY_FOR_NEXT_GOVERNED_STEP != WORK_FULLY_KNOWN`. A candidate Motive and desired outcome are necessary. A Human-owned decision that materially blocks **the current next step**—authority, privacy, safety, destructive effect, external mutation, cost, scope, or acceptance meaning—remains a blocker and is shown in Actions. Cheap, reversible details (filename, placeholder data, styling, layout) belong in candidate assumptions/ambiguities; they are not silently promoted to Human-approved facts and do not prevent forming Work. The WIC question evaluations preserve deferred information and determine whether it blocks the next step; Work Admission readiness only admits when current-step blockers are absent. Later steps may pause at the point where deferred information becomes material. This is not permission to guess irreversible or Human-owned decisions.

`按当前理解开始` means the Human authorizes Work formation using the latest exact assessment and basis, known constraints and the existing authority identity. It calls the existing governed Work Admission path; it is not a skip-refinement or blanket production authorization. `继续完善` returns focus to the Composer. When admitted, the normal automatic Steering activation may proceed; further Work refinement remains possible. No new schema, Work lifecycle enum, or persistence owner is introduced.

For the course-table example, the page, 8×5 table, grade level and invented data provide a candidate outcome and a reversible first implementation. Filename, decorative styling and conventional orientation need not trigger repeated ceremonial confirmations. Human product acceptance must still establish whether the real Provider and UI follow this path correctly; focused automated checks alone do not close that question.
