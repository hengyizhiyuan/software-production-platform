# Human Governance

## Human Agency First

Not every action requires manual confirmation. High-frequency, low-risk tasks may be executed automatically.

High-impact, high-risk, strategic, or uncertain tasks retain a Human Governance Point.

## Human Authority within Production Governance

The [State Foundation closure](../architecture/spg-state-foundation.md) clarifies **Human Authority Does Not Imply Runtime Bypass**. Human Agency First preserves meaningful authority over intent, direction, organizational constraints, risk ownership, high-impact exceptions, and final acceptance where policy requires it; it does not make a human an unrestricted production superuser.

Human decisions enter the governed transition flow through explicit authority records. Production Governance Runtime validates, admits, and performs authoritative SPG transitions. Human authority does not bypass production consistency, lineage, state-transition, or Commit rules.

Human / Policy Final Acceptance primarily targets an exact sealed Baseline Candidate, not each PWU by default. The [C closure](../architecture/spg-completion-trust.md) confirms that Acceptance is policy-driven, not universally manual. Human Agency First means Humans define and retain Authority boundaries; Required Authority must be satisfied.

Human, AI, Executor, Guardian, and other actors are governed participants with different Responsibility / Authority. Equal submission to production governance does not imply equal authority. Human strategic, risk, and acceptance authority remains intact.

## Distributed Responsibility and Governed Adjudication

**No Actor Owns Production Truth Alone.** Human, Planner, Executor, Guardian, Context Provider, and other participants contribute domain-owned decisions, proposals, facts, artifacts, or evidence. Production Governance Runtime adjudicates admission through explicit contracts, policy, and transition rules; it is not the sole creator or owner of all truth.

This is not majority voting. Human intent, strategic direction, constraints, risk ownership, exceptions, and applicable final acceptance retain their distinct authority. Human Authority does not bypass runtime consistency or allow observed production facts to be rewritten.

Physical provider consolidation does not collapse logical authority. The same model may supply multiple capabilities, but capability reuse does not authorize unilateral self-acceptance or Commit. Stronger models may receive policy-bounded autonomy, not unrestricted production authority.

Risk Acceptance changes governance permission, not Findings, Evidence, or observed facts. A governed Exception identifies the exact obligation, Candidate, authorized domain, rationale, scope, validity, and follow-up where applicable; it is not a hidden Runtime bypass. Acceptance and Exception may become stale when Candidate, Policy, or material basis changes.

For External Side Effects, Human Authority may grant scoped permission, accept risk, authorize Exception, or approve temporary divergence. It does not grant broad Executor privilege, bypass Runtime provenance / fencing, erase observed effects, rewrite Guardian Evidence, or silently admit observed reality into the Trusted Baseline.

See the [architecture principles](../architecture/architecture-principles.md), [Reconciliation & Recovery Closure](../architecture/spg-reconciliation-recovery.md), [Completion & Trust Closure](../architecture/spg-completion-trust.md), [Side-effect Governance Closure](../architecture/spg-side-effect-governance.md), and [Runtime Architecture Final Closure and Readiness](../architecture/spg-runtime-architecture-readiness.md). The Final Review is PASSED; Human Authority remains governed and does not acquire Runtime bypass.


## Controlled Implementation Governance

The [SPG Lite Runtime Implementation Contract](../architecture/spg-lite-runtime-implementation-contract.md) records Coding Readiness PASS and **AUTHORIZED FOR CONTROLLED IMPLEMENTATION**. This status permits only explicitly authorized, repository-grounded slices after Architecture Lead Reality Review; it does not authorize a specific slice or uncontrolled bulk implementation.

Conversational agreement is not execution Authority until admitted into a governed, versioned Source-of-Truth Artifact or Contract. Human Authority remains meaningful through explicit Production Intent, Governance Records, risk/Exception decisions, and Candidate authorization rather than raw chat dependencies or Runtime bypass.
