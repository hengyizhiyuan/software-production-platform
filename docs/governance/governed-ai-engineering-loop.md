# Governed AI Engineering Loop

## Human Governor

Responsible for:

- Goal
- Direction
- Constraint
- Acceptance
- Major Trade-off

## Design Lead AI

Responsible for:

- Architecture
- Design
- Task decomposition
- Progress coordination

## Repo-grounded Executor

Responsible for:

- Repository reality
- Implementation
- Verification execution

## Evidence / Artifact

Evidence and artifacts are the long-term source of facts. Conversation history is not the Source of Truth.
