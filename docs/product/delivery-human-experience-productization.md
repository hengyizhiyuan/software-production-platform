# Delivery Human Experience / Productization

Date: 2026-09-20

Status: **OPEN / NON-BLOCKING**

## Current reality

The current milestone proves the Delivery capability and acceptance lifecycle:

- an exact trusted artifact can be published and downloaded;
- an exact software manifest can start an isolated local Runtime;
- Runtime readiness and artifact identity are checked before software acceptance;
- the Human can record an immutable accept or request-changes decision; and
- Delivery acceptance remains distinct from Work completion and technical
  Verification.

The final Human review also showed that the page remains too oriented around
machine state, manifests, fingerprints, and technical evidence. Its information
hierarchy and interaction flow are not yet sufficiently productized for a formal
Watt experience. Capability correctness is accepted; presentation maturity is a
future product obligation.

## Future direction

Delivery should be optimized together with Watt's other formal product surfaces:

- Home;
- History / Works;
- Deliveries;
- Settings;
- Login / Register; and
- other formal product pages introduced through future product design.

That work should clarify the Human's current task, preview and decision sequence,
accepted versus pending state, and progressive disclosure of technical evidence.
It must preserve exact artifact identity, Runtime truth, Human Authority, and the
separation between technical completion and product acceptance.

This finding does not authorize a Delivery redesign and does not block the
current milestone closure.
